About UDSS

More than a school. A foundation.

The University of Benin Demonstration Secondary School was founded on 5 October 1981 and is situated at the University of Benin's Ugbowo Campus in Benin City.

The school combines academic development with character formation, providing an environment where students can broaden their knowledge, develop critical thinking and participate in curricular and co-curricular activities.

“Knowledge for Service.”
